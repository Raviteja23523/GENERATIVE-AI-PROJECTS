1.Question: How do I set up my laptop?
Role (employee/manager/it_admin/hr): hr

Answer: To set up your laptop, follow these steps:
1. Sign in using the temporary credentials provided by IT.
2. Change the temporary password.
3. Enroll the company device in the required device-management service.
4. Complete the security awareness training.

If you have any problems with laptop setup, you can contact the onboarding coordinator through the internal support channel for help.
Sources: onboarding_guide.md, support_escalation_guide.md, product_faq.md, product_faq.md


2.Question: How do I connect to the company VPN?
Role (employee/manager/it_admin/hr): manager

Answer: VPN access is requested through the approved access-request process described in the Security Policy. A VPN access request should identify: 
- The requesting user's role
- The requested resource
- The reason for access. The request must follow the approved access-request workflow.
Sources: onboarding_guide.md, security_policy.md, security_policy.md, product_faq.md
